The idea

One platform where teaching, learning, and paying finally live together.

Academia is an education marketplace and learning platform connecting students, teachers, and courses — combining discovery, enrollment, an internal wallet for fees and payouts, and academic tracking such as attendance and exams in a single role‑aware system.

How it works

From join request to teacher payout

STEP 1

Request

Student submits a join request for a course.

STEP 2

Approve

Teacher reviews and approves — low balance blocks approval automatically.

STEP 3

Deduct

The fee is deducted from the student's wallet, atomically.

STEP 4

Split

Commission is calculated from the active rate and logged to the ledger.

STEP 5

Pay out

The teacher's wallet is credited net of commission; withdrawal follows on request.

Steps 3 and 4 are one atomic transaction — the fee deduction and the teacher's net credit succeed or fail together, so wallet balances and the revenue ledger never drift apart.

Business model

We earn only when a teacher earns

Academia's revenue engine is a transparent, admin‑configurable commission on every paid enrollment that moves through the platform wallet — recorded to a dedicated revenue ledger every step of the way.

Top‑up
Verified bank transfer into the wallet
Enrollment
Fee deducted, commission calculated
Payout
Teacher withdraws net earnings

Why a commission, not a subscription

  • Teachers pay nothing to list a course — zero upfront risk to join.
  • Academia only earns when a real, paid enrollment happens.
  • Every commission is logged in a ledger both sides can audit.
  • Aligns platform growth directly with teacher and student success.
Core: enrollment commission Future: premium placement Future: analytics for teachers

Unit economics — the framework, not a forecast

How one enrollment turns into revenue

Enrollments

Number of paid join requests approved through the wallet in a period.

× Average fee

The course or session price the student pays, set by each teacher.

× Commission %

The admin‑configurable platform rate applied at the moment of deduction.

= Platform revenue

Logged automatically to the revenue ledger — no separate invoicing step.

Why we're showing a formula, not a forecast

We don't have real enrollment volume yet — the platform hasn't launched, so any revenue number today would be invented, not measured. What's real is the mechanism above: near‑zero marginal cost per transaction, revenue that scales directly with enrollments rather than headcount, and a cost base (engineering, hosting, verification staff) that grows far more slowly than transaction volume once the payment gateway replaces manual bank‑transfer checks.

Four real risks, and how we're handling each

Disintermediation

A teacher and student could exchange contact info and transact outside the platform to avoid the commission. Mitigation: the wallet, attendance record, materials hosting, and parent dashboard are workflow value a side deal doesn't replicate — staying on‑platform stays more convenient than leaving it.

Manual‑transfer fraud

Today's top‑up relies on a human checking a bank receipt. Mitigation: every verification is logged with an admin ID and timestamp, balances never move without approval, and an automated payment gateway is next on the roadmap specifically to close this gap.

Chicken‑and‑egg supply/demand

No students without teachers, no teachers without students. Mitigation: we seed supply first — independent teachers and small centers get the tools free during onboarding, and their existing students migrate in as first demand.

A funded competitor moves faster

Noon Academy or Orcas could extend toward our model. Mitigation: neither is optimized around wallet + commission ledger + parent dashboard + academic tracking together — pivoting a funded product's core is organizationally slower than a focused build.

Winning students

Why a student signs up — and stays

Students don't need another app to check. They need one place where finding a teacher, paying safely, and seeing their own progress finally fit together.

  • Browse before you commit — compare teachers, subjects, and prices in one catalog instead of asking around.
  • Pay once, use everywhere — a single wallet covers every course, no separate transfer per teacher.
  • See your own progress — attendance and exam results live in your account, not a teacher's notebook.
  • Trust, not guesswork — enrollment is confirmed, blocked from duplicating, and receipted.

Winning teachers

"Why should I give up a cut of my fee?"

What the teacher gives up

A transparent, admin‑governed commission — calculated and logged automatically on every enrollment fee that clears through the wallet. Never a fixed lock‑in, always visible in the ledger.

What the teacher gets back

New students — discovered through the marketplace, not just their existing circle.

Zero collection hassle — no chasing transfers or reconciling who actually paid.

A real dashboard — join requests, rosters, and payouts, all in one place.

The pitch is simple: the commission is the price of demand generation and automated, verified collection — work every independent teacher currently does themselves, for free, badly.
