V4 — FULL IMAGE GENERATION SPECIFICATION

PURPOSE
-------
This pack converts the supplied Academia UX/UI documentation into detailed,
copy-ready AI image-generation specifications.

IMPORTANT
---------
- Generated product UI is CONCEPT ONLY unless replaced by real Academia screenshots.
- Generated portraits must NOT be presented as real founders, teachers, students, users, or testimonials.
- Real product evidence should ultimately come from the actual product.
- Every image has a defined purpose, style, composition, aspect ratio, lighting,
  color direction, prompt, and negative prompt.
- Do not generate an image merely because a section has empty space.

============================================================

STYLE

Human Editorial Geometry.

Influences:

- Restrained Playful Geometric design
- Modern Arabic EdTech
- Product-led visual storytelling
- Quietly expressive composition
- Deliberate asymmetry

FEEL
----

contemporary, culturally appropriate, premium without luxury,
playful without childishness.

BRAND COLORS
------------

Azure #3E8EDE
Amber #F0A62E
Emerald #17A672
Violet #9A6AD6

Use navy as grounding, amber as the primary accent, azure/emerald as
secondary accents, and violet rarely. Do not artificially tint photographs
with brand colors.

DO NOT TURN THIS INTO
---------------------
Memphis chaos, generic Gen-Z SaaS, childish education art, generic AI
landing-page imagery, Dribbble dashboard showcases, glassmorphism, gradient
mesh, sticker UI, excessive blobs, confetti, or glossy 3D.

02 — GLOBAL PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE
============================================================

STYLE NAME
----------
Academia Human Editorial Photography

CHARACTER
---------
Photorealistic documentary/editorial educational photography.
Authentic rather than commercial stock.

CAMERA
------
35–50mm equivalent.
Natural perspective.
3/4 observational angles.
Medium environmental portraits.
Moderate depth of field.

LIGHTING
--------
Natural window light or warm desk-lamp/ambient evening light.
Soft directional light.
Realistic shadows.
Natural highlight rolloff.

AVOID
-----
Studio-white lighting, extreme rim light, nightclub colors, movie lighting,
excessive bloom, excessive lens flare, extreme bokeh, fisheye distortion,
wide-angle facial distortion.

SKIN
----
Natural skin texture, realistic complexion, minimal retouching.

ENVIRONMENT
-----------
Believable Palestinian educational/home context.
Avoid luxury interiors, futuristic classrooms, Silicon Valley offices,
generic Western school stereotypes.

COMPOSITION
-----------
Editorial asymmetry.
Intentional negative space.
Strong subject hierarchy.
When paired with Arabic RTL copy, reserve a clean area for the text and
never place the face beneath the text.
