ACADEMIA — IMAGE PROMPTS & VISUAL GENERATION GUIDE
V3
===============================================================

PURPOSE
-------
This file consolidates the image-related directions found across the 11
Academia UX/UI audit documents supplied in the ZIP.

IMPORTANT:
Most of the source documents do NOT contain AI image-generation prompts.
They contain UX/UI "Capture Briefs" describing real product screenshots,
real photography, or real team photography.

Therefore this file separates:

1. SOURCE-DERIVED ASSET BRIEFS
   What the original Academia UX/UI documents actually require.

2. IMPROVED GENERATION PROMPTS
   AI-generation versions created from those briefs where generation is
   appropriate.

3. VISUAL STYLE / CONSISTENCY RULES
   Shared rules that should be applied across generated imagery.

4. DO-NOT-GENERATE ASSETS
   Assets that should come from the actual product or real people instead.

The goal is NOT to generate everything with AI.

===============================================================
01 — MASTER VISUAL STYLE FOR GENERATED PHOTOGRAPHY
===============================================================

STYLE NAME
-----------
Academia Human Editorial Photography

STYLE CHARACTER
---------------
Authentic documentary-style educational photography.
Editorial rather than commercial stock photography.
Natural, observational, human, calm, youthful, culturally appropriate,
premium but not luxurious.

VISUAL CHARACTER
----------------
- Realistic photography
- Documentary/editorial feeling
- Natural human behavior
- Subtle imperfections
- Warm evening interior lighting
- Slightly desaturated color grade
- Deep navy / warm amber environmental relationship
- Natural skin tones
- Soft contrast
- Controlled highlights
- Gentle shadow detail
- No glossy advertising look
- No artificial "perfect student" pose
- No exaggerated cinematic effects

CAMERA LANGUAGE
---------------
- 3/4 or observational angles
- Slightly above eye level when appropriate
- Medium shots or environmental portraits
- Natural depth of field
- Subject integrated into environment
- Avoid extreme wide-angle distortion
- Avoid overly shallow depth of field that destroys context
- Avoid straight-on corporate headshots unless the asset specifically requires
  a team portrait

LIGHTING
--------
- Warm domestic / educational interior light
- Desk lamp + ambient evening light where appropriate
- Soft directional light
- Natural shadow falloff
- No bright white studio lighting
- No glossy commercial lighting
- No dramatic movie lighting
- No excessive rim light

COLOR
-----
Base visual grade:
- Deep navy environment: #0A0F1C
- Warm amber accent: #F0A62E
- Optional subtle azure: #3E8EDE
- Optional subtle emerald: #17A672
- Violet should be rare.

Do not force these colors into the photograph unnaturally.
The photo should remain photorealistic.

The site UI may carry the brand colors; the photograph does not need to
contain literal amber/blue/green objects.

COMPOSITION
-----------
- Editorial asymmetry
- Intentional negative space
- Clear subject hierarchy
- Composition must support Arabic RTL layouts
- Never place important facial detail where headline text will sit
- Do not automatically center the subject
- Avoid symmetrical stock-photo composition

AUTHENTICITY
------------
The image should look like a real photograph taken in Palestine / the
local educational context, not a generic international stock photo.

Avoid:
- Silicon Valley aesthetics
- Western corporate classroom stereotypes
- exaggerated "happy student" expressions
- fake futuristic education interfaces
- unrealistic holograms
- generic AI classroom scenes

===============================================================

fake dashboard, fake statistics, fake testimonials, fake text, random

IMPROVED AI GENERATION PROMPT
-----------------------------

Create a photorealistic editorial documentary photograph of a Palestinian
Tawjihi student, approximately 17–18 years old, studying seriously at home
during the evening.

The student is naturally focused on an open notebook at a simple study desk,
with a laptop or phone nearby. The student is looking down at the study
material, not at the camera. The behavior should feel candid and observational,
as if captured during a real study session rather than posed for an
advertisement.

Use a slightly elevated 3/4 camera angle, medium environmental portrait,
natural perspective, realistic anatomy, authentic skin texture, and subtle
depth of field while keeping enough environmental context visible.

The home study environment should feel believable and modest: notebook,
pens, textbooks, a practical desk, and a nearby personal device. Avoid
luxury interiors and avoid staged "perfect student" compositions.

Use warm evening illumination from a desk lamp combined with soft ambient
room light. Preserve realistic shadow detail and natural skin tones. The
lighting should feel calm and intimate, not cinematic or dramatic.

Compose the subject toward one side of the frame and intentionally preserve
clean negative space on the opposite side for Arabic RTL headline content.
Do not place important visual details in the negative-space area.

The image should feel culturally and geographically appropriate to Palestine,
authentic, youthful, calm, focused, and human.

Color grade: subtly warm and slightly desaturated, natural skin tones,
soft contrast, restrained highlights, visually compatible with Academia's
deep navy #0A0F1C and amber #F0A62E identity without artificially tinting
the skin or environment.

No text, no logos, no generated UI, no futuristic interface.

OUTPUT:
Photorealistic editorial photography, vertical 4:5 composition, high detail,
clean composition, realistic proportions.

minimal flat-to-soft dimensional treatment, clean vector-inspired forms,
restrained Playful Geometric vocabulary, no Memphis chaos.

22 — GLOBAL HUMAN-PHOTOGRAPHY CONSISTENCY
===============================================================

These rules are taken from the supplied Academia visual direction and should
be applied to every future human photograph.

COLOR GRADE
-----------
- warm
- slightly desaturated
- natural skin tones
- compatible with ink-950 / amber-primary
- no cold blue cast
- no oversaturation

LIGHTING
--------
- warm indoor / evening where context permits
- soft directional light
- realistic shadows
- avoid bright white studio light

PEOPLE
------
Use subjects that feel culturally appropriate to the Palestinian educational
context.

Do not make every image look like the same person, same wardrobe, or same
stock-photo session.

CAMERA
------
Prefer:
- 3/4
- observational
- candid
- environmental

Avoid:
- direct-to-camera advertising portraits
- artificial smiles
- rigid corporate poses

TREATMENT
---------
- no glass frame
- no heavy drop shadow
- no artificial 3D perspective
- no decorative border unless composition requires it
- allow images to breathe into the page

===============================================================
23 — IMAGE PLACEMENT RULES
===============================================================

Do not automatically use:

rounded card + shadow + centered image

Instead choose intentionally between:

- edge crop
- editorial crop
- asymmetric frame
- partial overlap
- full-bleed image
- product screenshot
- masked geometry
- image behind text only if contrast remains excellent

Blurred image backgrounds should be rare.

Do not put a blurred image behind every section.

===============================================================
27 — FINAL ACADEMIA IMAGE PROMPT TEMPLATE
===============================================================

Use this structure whenever a NEW generated image is approved:

[SUBJECT]
Who/what is shown.

[CONTEXT]
Where the subject exists and why.

[ACTION]
What the subject is doing.

[CAMERA]
Angle, focal length feel, framing, perspective.

[LIGHTING]
Direction, temperature, intensity.

[COMPOSITION]
Subject placement, negative space, RTL considerations.

[COLOR]
Natural grade + Academia-compatible accents.

[ART DIRECTION]
Editorial / documentary / geometric / educational.

[AUTHENTICITY]
Local/cultural context and realistic imperfections.

[OUTPUT]
Aspect ratio, resolution, background requirements.

[NEGATIVE]
Stock-photo behavior, fake UI, text, logos, excessive effects, etc.

===============================================================
28 — CRITICAL RULE FOR IMAGE TOOLS
===============================================================

Do not let the image generator decide the art direction.

The approved section design decides:

1. Why the image exists.
2. What the image communicates.
3. Where the image appears.
4. What composition it needs.
5. What aspect ratio it requires.
6. What visual style it uses.

Only then should the image tool generate the asset.

===============================================================
30 — FINAL ART-DIRECTION CHECK
===============================================================

Before approving any generated image, ask:

[ ] Does it communicate a real product/user idea?
[ ] Is it specific to Academia?
[ ] Does it fit Arabic RTL composition?
[ ] Does it feel culturally appropriate?
[ ] Does it look editorial rather than stock?
[ ] Is the subject behavior believable?
[ ] Is the lighting consistent with the visual language?
[ ] Is the color grade consistent?
[ ] Does it leave the required negative space?
[ ] Does it avoid competing with typography?
[ ] Does it avoid generic AI aesthetics?
[ ] Does it avoid fake product evidence?
[ ] Is the image actually necessary?
[ ] Would removing it make the section clearer?

If the final answer is "no" to the last question, remove the image.
